- 0x313/0x314/0x315 (16.7Hz) = NGHI LA DANH SACH VAT THE RADAR:
  * moi tin 2 nua 4 byte, header o byte1 va byte5.
  * header 0x81 = o trong. Cac gia tri khac: 0x313: 04/06/08/0A, 0x314: 0C/0E/10/12,
    0x315: 14/16/18/1A (bit7 bat/tat luan phien) -> ~12 o vat the, chi so = (header>>1)&0x3F.
  * Tach theo header: |r| voi dRel len 0.53-0.73 (vd 0x314 byte5=0x12: r=-0.73;
    0x315 byte1=0x16: r=-0.63). Chua tim ra truong vRel (|r|<0.4).
  * He so am / offset la -> chua biet ma hoa (co the do o vat the khong luon la xe truoc).
- 0x326/0x327/0x329 (12.5Hz): nhieu entropy, nghi la vat the CAMERA (fusion). |r|~0.33. 0x328, 0x460, 0x181, 0x29C gan hang so.
- 0x2AC (3.5Hz) |r|~0.38, chua ro.
